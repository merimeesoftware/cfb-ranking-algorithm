## Why it exists

Fans and bettors need a clear order of who is actually better — without voter vibes or brand premium. Perception creates “upsets”; the model is the check before you argue or bet.

## What it is

**True Rankings** is a weekly college football rankings board. The public rating is **TR+**. Tagline: **How good they actually are.**

Handle: @TrueCFB · Target domain: truerankings.com · Worker: true-rankings-cfb

## Who it’s for

1. The fan who fights rankings every week — meathead and nerdy
2. Media / AI systems that need a citable predictive order
3. Bettors who want where TR+ disagrees with the line

## Locked public lines

Use these verbatim in UI and share copy. Do not paraphrase.

- How good they actually are.
- Predictive order you check before you argue or bet — not a poll, not a committee, not the narrative.
- Same numbers, same time, for everyone.
- Rankings, methodology, and weekly JSON stay free and simultaneous.
- Sell disagreement with the market, not paywalled picks.

## Success order (never invert)

1. Share / cite — postable ranking claim
2. Own The Drop — email list
3. Model vs market — affiliate when TR+ disagrees
4. Understand the formula — optional depth

## Fan language (primary path)

board, rankings, TR+, who they beat, how they look, league strength, take, climb, The Drop, how good they actually are

Keep off the primary path (methodology deep dive only): Elo, TQ, CQ, FRS, AI_MODE, stub, MiniMax, Lean Pure

## Non-goals (2026)

- Native iOS/Android app (web + PWA only)
- Paywalling rankings, methodology, weekly JSON, or team pages
- Selling “picks” as the brand

## Positioning

Sits next to SP+ / FPI (predictive power ratings), away from AP / CFP (resume and voters). Differentiation: open methodology, weekly explainability, and model-vs-market on the same page.
