How the order works

Approved order

Sorted by how they look, high to low.

Heat's weight is 0. Record, record quality, Heat, and early-season are columns.

Old blend, still what the code publishes

0.80 × how they look + 0.15 × résumé score + 0.05 × league strength.

The parts are not scaled to 0–100 before weighting. On the 2026 week 10 file the old blend matches the stored score. 127 of 138 teams sit in a different place than how they look. The average gap is 5.99 places.

How they look is the Elo rating in the code.

Step size
50. A postseason game multiplies it by 0.65, so 32.5.
Margin
The natural log of the margin plus 1. There is no cap.
Home field
In the expectation only: 50 in the regular season, 20 in the postseason, 0 on a neutral field.
Surprise step
A win over a team rated more than 150 points higher multiplies the step by 1.18. A Group of 5 win over a Power 4 team multiplies it by 1.12. They stack. The product is not capped at 1.18.
Who plays whom
Power 4 vs Power 4 is 1.0. Power 4 vs Group of 5 is 0.8. Group of 5 vs Group of 5 is 0.65. FBS vs FCS is 0.2. Anything else is 0.1.
Ceiling
No winner's rating goes above 1,850. The loser still drops the full step.
Opening number
A Power 4 team starts at 1,500, other FBS at 1,200, and FCS at 900, blended with 0.70 of last year's final score and 0.30 of the year before.
Fade and early
The opening blend fades by max(0, 0.7 × (12 − week) / 11). Week 1 keeps 0.7. Week 10 keeps 0.127. Week 12 keeps 0. Early is on when a team has fewer than 3 games. On 2026 week 1 every team is early. Indiana led that file at 1-0, how they look 1,827.4. On week 10 the flag is off for all 138 teams.
Heat
Weight 0. Three definitions were tested. Every weight above 0 fit worse than how they look alone on 2019–2022. No Heat number is stored, so the column is empty.
