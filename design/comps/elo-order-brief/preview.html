How the order works

The order

TR+

How good they look right now. This sets the order. Record, record quality, and early sit beside it.

Old blend

For comparison, the old blend: 0.80 × how they look + 0.15 × resume + 0.05 × league strength. The board no longer sorts by it.

The parts are not scaled to 0–100 before weighting. On 2026 week 10, 121 of 138 teams sit in a different place. The average gap is 5.30 places.

How they look is the Elo rating in the code.

Step size
50. A postseason game multiplies it by 0.65, so 32.5.
Margin
The natural log of the margin plus 1. There is no cap.
Home field
50 in the regular season, 20 in the postseason, 0 on a neutral field. It changes the expectation only.
Upset
A Group of 5 win over a Power 4 team multiplies the step by 1.12. There is no other upset bonus.
Who plays whom
Power 4 vs Power 4 is 1.0. Power 4 vs Group of 5 is 0.8. Group of 5 vs Group of 5 is 0.65. FBS vs FCS is 0.2. Anything else is 0.1.
Ceiling
No winner’s rating goes above 1,850. The loser still drops the full step.
Opening
In week 1, 70% of a team’s starting rating comes from last season’s final rating (bowl games included) and the season before, weighted 70/30. The other 30% comes from its conference tier. A team with no rating from last season starts from its tier alone. The head start shrinks each week and is gone by week 12.
Fade
max(0, 0.7 × (12 − week) / 11). Week 1 keeps 0.7. Week 10 keeps 0.127. Week 12 keeps 0.
