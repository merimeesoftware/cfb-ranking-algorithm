# PRODUCT.md — True Rankings

## What it is

**True Rankings** is a weekly college football rankings board. The public rating is **TR+**. Tagline: **How good they actually are.**

## Who it’s for

1. The fan who fights rankings every week — meathead *and* nerdy  
2. Media / AI systems that need a citable order  

## StoryBrand frame (locked)

| Role | Answer |
|------|--------|
| Hero | The fan |
| Guide | True Rankings |
| Problem | Who is actually better — without voter vibes or brand premium |
| Plan | Open the board → find your team → share/cite |
| Primary CTAs | Share Top 25 · Copy citation |
| Success | Cite the model → win the argument → optionally understand the formula |

## Success order

1. **Share / cite** — postable ranking claim  
2. **Understand the formula** — optional depth  

## Fan language (primary path)

Use: board, rankings, TR+, who they beat, how they look, league strength, take, climb, how good they actually are.

Keep off the primary path: Elo, TQ, CQ, FRS, `AI_MODE`, stub, MiniMax (methodology deep dive only).

## Surfaces

| Surface | Job |
|---------|-----|
| Home / week page | This week’s true order |
| The Board | Who’s where; find your team |
| Team page | Take → path to climb → why → math |
| Matchup | Head-to-head of published TR+ ratings |
| How it works | How the board decides, then the math |

## Hard rules

- Rankings, methodology, and weekly JSON stay free and simultaneous  
- No native app in 2026 (web + PWA)  
- No email signup, no odds, no spreads, no picks
